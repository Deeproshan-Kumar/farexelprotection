Senior Automotive Detailer

  • 1+ year experience
  • Full-time
  • Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

We are looking for an experienced and detail-oriented Senior Automotive Detailer to join our team. The ideal candidate will have strong automotive detailing skills, a passion for vehicle care, and the ability to maintain high-quality standards while working efficiently.

Job Overview

  • Position: Senior Automotive Detailer
  • Employment Type: Full-Time
  • Department: Vehicle Care & Detailing
  • Experience: 3+ years in automotive detailing
  • Location: [Location]
  • Reports To: Detailing Manager / Service Manager

Job Description

As a Senior Automotive Detailer, you will be responsible for delivering high-quality interior and exterior vehicle detailing services. You will inspect vehicles, identify detailing requirements, perform advanced cleaning and restoration procedures, and ensure every vehicle meets the company's quality standards before handover.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform comprehensive interior and exterior automotive detailing.
  • Wash, clean, polish, wax, and protect vehicle exteriors.
  • Perform interior vacuuming, shampooing, steam cleaning, and conditioning.
  • Apply ceramic coatings, paint protection products, and other protective treatments as required.
  • Clean and restore wheels, tires, glass, trim, and other vehicle surfaces.
  • Inspect vehicles before and after detailing to ensure quality standards are met.
  • Identify stains, scratches, paint imperfections, and other detailing requirements.
  • Operate detailing equipment, pressure washers, polishers, extractors, steam cleaners, and other tools safely.
  • Use automotive cleaning chemicals and products according to manufacturer guidelines.
  • Maintain detailing tools, equipment, work areas, and vehicle bays in clean and safe condition.
  • Guide junior detailers and provide practical training when required.

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • 3+ years of professional automotive detailing experience.
  • Strong knowledge of automotive cleaning, polishing, and detailing techniques.
  • Hands-on experience with paint correction and surface polishing.
  • Experience using professional detailing tools and equipment.
  • Ability to identify different vehicle surfaces and select appropriate cleaning methods.
  • Good understanding of vehicle safety and proper handling procedures.
  • Valid driving license is an advantage.

Key Skills

  • Automotive Detailing
  • Paint Correction
  • Machine Polishing
  • Interior Detailing
  • Exterior Detailing
  • Ceramic Coating Application
  • Vehicle Inspection
  • Equipment Handling
  • Quality Control
  • Team Leadership
  • Time Management
  • Attention to Detail

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary based on experience and skills.
  • Opportunity to work with a professional automotive team.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Performance-based incentives.
  • Professional and supportive work environment.
  • Opportunities for career growth and advancement.

Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is passionate about automobiles, takes pride in delivering exceptional workmanship, and has a strong eye for detail. You should be reliable, physically fit, safety-conscious, and capable of working independently as well as leading or supporting a detailing team.

How to Apply

If you have the required experience and are passionate about automotive detailing, submit your updated resume along with your relevant experience and contact details.
